Drive & Deliver

Delivery driving prototype — Deliver products around the map.

Platform: Windows
Engine/Tech: C# (Unity)
Status: Prototype
Duration: 7 weeks
Team: 1 Developer

Game objective

Drive & Deliver is a prototype focused on deliveries. Drive through city streets, stay on the road and deliver packages to their destinations.

Gallery

Description

This prototype explores movement mechanics, delivery objectives and simple scoring. The goal is to reach drop-off points quickly while managing traffic and hazards.

Wheel collider & Mass

Notes on the vehicle setup: wheel collider configuration and rigidbody mass tuning used to balance handling and responsiveness.

Wheelcollider transform will be on the location of the wheel, so the driving feels good /better and visual seems to be more realistic.
