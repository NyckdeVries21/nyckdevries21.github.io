Description

A prototype card game where you can play against a bot. You can use cards by clicking on the card. When you click on the card there will spawn an object. Heal/ defend/ attack object, depends what card u sellected. There is "no end" until you are dead/defeated by the enemy.

CardInventory System

Images of it:

Made a list for all the cards and choose random a card to put in the inventory. In this case they put the card on the card location ( used this for every card location). When you click on the card it will spawn an object and remove the card from the inventory.
